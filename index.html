What the workshop taught me

Before code, I spent 3.5 years as an apprentice auto electrician doing auto-electrical, mechanical, electrical and plumbing work. A lot of it carries straight over:

  • Fault-finding Tracing a dead circuit one connection at a time takes the same patience as debugging code.
  • Real customers I'm used to explaining a problem in plain language to someone who just wants it fixed.
  • Finishing the job A vehicle isn't done until it starts. An app isn't done until it's deployed and tested.
