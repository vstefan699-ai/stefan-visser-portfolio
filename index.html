What the workshop taught me

Before code, I spent three and a half years as an apprentice auto electrician, doing auto-electrical, mechanical, electrical and plumbing work. Much of it carries straight over:

  • Fault-finding Tracing a dead circuit one connection at a time takes the same patience as debugging code.
  • Adaptability The work spanned four trades, so I'm used to picking up unfamiliar systems quickly.
  • Learning by doing An apprenticeship is learning on the job. I learn new tools the same way: build, test, fix.
