About me

From fixing wiring looms to wiring up APIs.

From the workshop to the web

I'm Stefan, a junior full-stack developer based in Kuruman in the Northern Cape. Before I wrote a line of code, I spent three and a half years as an apprentice auto electrician, doing auto-electrical, mechanical, electrical and plumbing work. Alongside the job I completed my N2 in mathematics, engineering science, motor electrical trade theory and industrial electronics.

Eventually, I hit a plateau. I'd learned what the job had to teach me, and I wanted a career where the learning never stops. Software is exactly that: there's always a new tool to learn, a better way to build something, a harder problem to solve. It's a career I'm in for the long haul, and the fault-finding mindset from the workshop came with me. These days, the faults are in the logic instead of the loom.

In January 2026 I joined Code College's Web Developer Bootcamp, where I learned the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React and Node.js) as well as Python and Django. My projects include ShopLog, a vehicle service tracker that an independent mechanic uses for his own work, and FitTrack Pro, a gym and personal-training management app built with Django and PostgreSQL.

Now I'm looking for my first junior developer role, somewhere I can keep learning from experienced developers while building things people actually use.
